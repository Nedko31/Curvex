Privacy Policy – Curvex

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. About the app

Curvex is a currency and cryptocurrency converter. It does not require an account.

2. Information stored on your device

Curvex stores your selected app language locally on your device using Local Storage. The app does not send the amount you enter or your currency selections to a Curvex server.

3. Exchange-rate and market-price services

Curvex retrieves currency reference rates from Frankfurter and cryptocurrency market prices from Binance public market-data endpoints. When these services are contacted, they may receive technical information such as your IP address and request metadata according to their own privacy policies. Curvex does not send your entered conversion amount as part of these requests.

Rates and prices are indicative and may be delayed or change. Check with your financial or trading provider before making a transaction.

4. Advertising

Curvex uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google and its partners may process device information, advertising identifiers, and other data as described in Google's privacy and advertising policies. Ad requests and personalized advertising are subject to applicable consent and device settings.

5. Consent

Where required, Curvex requests advertising consent through Google's consent tools before requesting ads. You can also manage advertising and privacy settings through your Android device and the options provided by Google.

6. Children's privacy

Curvex is not designed specifically for children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
